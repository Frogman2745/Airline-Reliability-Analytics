Interpretation and limits

This is a descriptive portfolio analysis. The displayed on-time rate excludes cancellations, diversions, and missing arrival status from its denominator. It is not a claim about causes, service improvements, or exact agreement with every published BTS metric.

The downloaded source months reconcile to 1,158,590 flight rows across 20 carrier-month records. The report is a static local snapshot, not a live feed. Raw source files and transformation scripts are documented in the project README.
